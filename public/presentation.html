The Challenge

The Conflict: Crop Sunlight vs. Solar Energy

Traditional static solar arrays create severe compromises on working farmland.

🌱 Crop Sunlight Needs

  • Sun-Starvation: Fixed solar arrays cast permanent shadows, depriving crops of essential PAR light.
  • Stunted Yields: Up to 40% yield drop in high-light crops like tomatoes, maize, and chillies.
  • Damp Microclimates: Trapped humidity beneath unmoving panels causes fungal disease and mildew.
  • Farmer Hesitation: Landowners fear solar installations will compromise their food security.

☀️ Solar Energy Demands

  • Sub-Optimal Angles: Fixed mounts capture peak irradiation for only a brief midday window (25% lost power).
  • Weather Vulnerability: Heavy hailstorms and severe wind gusts crack static glass surfaces.
  • Land Competition: Traditional solar farms lock up fertile agricultural soil for decades.
  • Grid Curtailment: Inflexible generation without smart battery synchronization wastes clean energy.
💡 The Core Dilemma: Agriculture needs open sunlight. Solar needs full exposure. How do we achieve both?
Our Solution

Sun-Starved Tracker: 4 Core Pillars

An intelligent, closed-loop agrivoltaic management ecosystem built for farmers.

1. AI Crop Health & PAR Monitoring

Monitors Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR), soil moisture, and crop canopy coverage. When crops are light-deficient, panels tilt away to allow filtered sunlight directly to root beds.

2. Dynamic Dual-Axis Kinematics

Smart hydraulic linear actuators adjust panel tilt (0° to 75°) and compass azimuth. Maximizes clean kilowatt-hours when crops are saturated, and provides cooling shade during scorching heatwaves.

3. Smart Battery & Energy Optimizer

Oversees dual LiFePO4 battery banks (SoC, cell health, charge cycles). Balances farm pump consumption, on-site storage, and profitable grid export without power interruptions.

4. Automated Safety Stowing & Alerts

Autonomous storm response: stows flat at 0° in high winds to reduce drag (-86%), angles at 30° during rain for guided water harvesting, and sends instant voice/SMS alerts in 6 languages.

System Workflow

Simple 4-Step Closed-Loop Flow

Automated sensing, AI intelligence, and precision mechanical actuation.

STEP 1
📡 Sense

IoT ground & sky sensors measure soil moisture, PAR sunlight flux, temperature, humidity, and real-time weather forecasts.

➔
STEP 2
🧠 Analyze

AI Decision Engine calculates the trade-off curve between crop photosynthesis requirements and solar generation targets.

➔
STEP 3
⚙️ Actuate

Motor actuators gently tilt elevated panels to the optimal angle (e.g., 15° morning boost, 35° solar track, or 0° storm stow).

➔
STEP 4
⚖️ Balance

Crops receive necessary light to flourish, clean electricity is stored in farm batteries, and grid export is scheduled profitably.

⚡ Fully Autonomous Closed Loop: Re-evaluates every 15 minutes without requiring farmer manual intervention.
